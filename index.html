About Me

I built this portfolio using the web development skills I learned during my college years. While I gained valuable knowledge, I realized that I wasn’t yet confident enough to pursue it as a full-time career. Because of this, I explored opportunities outside my field and began my professional journey as a Customer Service Representative.

I performed well in that role and worked there for six months. The job itself was manageable and rewarding, but the daily commute became challenging, which eventually led me to move on. I then looked for opportunities closer to home and became a Pharmacy Assistant, where I continued to develop my work ethic and interpersonal skills.

Later on, I came across an inspiring story about someone who became a successful Virtual Assistant. That moment sparked my interest in remote work. I began learning more about the field, investing in the necessary skills, and understanding both the advantages and challenges of working as a VA.

Through this journey, I discovered that being a Virtual Assistant aligns perfectly with my strengths and goals. Today, I am committed to building a career in this field and continuously improving myself to deliver value and support to clients.
